Every D1 team, ranked 1 to 361 — sortable, filterable by conference.

Real 2025–26 season data pulled from ESPN box scores, updated daily. Adj O, Adj D, Net Rtg, and Tempo are computed right here in the page — an iterative opponent-adjustment model (same spirit as Torvik/Pomeroy-style ratings) run once over the full season's box scores, so a win only counts as much as the opponent turns out to deserve. Click any column header to sort — the Rk column always shows each team's position in the current sorted, filtered list. Use Games and Opponent rank below to window the shooting/four-factor columns, Record, and Rk to a slice of the season — Adj O, Adj D, Net Rtg, Tempo, and everything in the Havoc/AST%/Luck/WAB/SOS/Radar group always stay season-long, since none of those can be meaningfully recomputed from a game subset. Every column's color scale, though, always reflects whichever teams are currently on screen — filter to one conference and the shading re-ranks within just that conference. Click a team to open its full dashboard.

Havoc, WAB, Luck, and Radar are explained in full on each team's own page; short version: Havoc = steals + blocks forced per 100 opponent possessions, WAB is an approximate Wins Above Bubble, Luck flags teams over/under-performing their point differential, and Radar flags teams whose numbers are notably better than the level of competition that's actually seen them. Switch to Player Leaderboard below to sort every rostered player in the country at once, Conferences for a conference-strength view, or Compare Teams for a side-by-side breakdown of any two teams.

Four factors, KPI cards, and Player Stats all reflect the filters above (default: full season), including Player(s): pick one or more and set them to "Played (in)" or "Did not play (out)" to see how the team's numbers shift with a player available vs. missing -- e.g. to gauge an injury's impact. "All selected" requires every picked player to match; "Any selected" needs just one. Overall rank, Adj. Offense, Adj. Defense, and Tempo come from the same opponent-adjusted ridge model as the Projections tab (fitted in the build on every D1 game, home court fitted, neutral sites from the schedule; once the projected season is under way, results plus the preseason projection), and are not affected by any filter, player or otherwise — that model's output can't be meaningfully recomputed from a game subset. The identity blurb up top, and Luck / WAB / Havoc Rate / Assist Rate / Quad Record below, are all season-long for the same reason. WAB (Wins Above Bubble) and the Quad Record use OUR rank, not the NCAA committee's official NET or the committee's own review of the Quad cut lines each year -- treat both as a close approximation, not an official number. Luck is wins above what points-for/points-against (a standard Pythagorean formula, D1 games only) would predict -- a helpful regression flag, not a judgment; an "unlucky" team isn't a bad one. Team Profile ranks and the 3PT Rate / TOV% / eFG% KPI ranks ARE recomputed live among all D1 teams, but only under the Location/Games/Opponent-rank/Conference filters -- a roster is specific to this team, so the Player(s) filter narrows this team's own numbers without changing what it's being ranked against. Each Team Profile row is shaded by that same live percentile. In the Game Log, click a date for that game's full recap (box scores, game flow, what decided it, standout performances), or an opponent's name to jump to their own dashboard (when they're a tracked D1 team). ORtg/DRtg are per-game estimates (points per 100 possessions, from that game's own box score), shaded by this team's own percentile across the games currently shown — not the season-long, opponent-adjusted Adj. Offense/Adj. Defense above, so don't expect them to match exactly. Each four-factor/shooting cell shows Team / Opponent for that single game, with whichever side is better in bold; unlike ORtg/DRtg, these aren't color-shaded, to keep the table from turning into 16 competing colors per row. Player Stats shows up to 12 players (the whole roster, if fewer than 12 have box-score data), sorted by MPG -- GP, MPG, PPG, RPG, OREB, APG, SPG, BPG, TOPG, FGM/FGA, 3FGM/3FGA, FTM/FTA, shooting splits, and the player value stats (OBPM/DBPM/BPM, VORP, wins above replacement, Game Score per 40, each split offense/defense/total) -- each shaded by percentile within the roster currently shown. Click a player to open her full player profile page. The Shooting by Zone tab shows the same players' makes and attempts (per game, or totals with Season Total), FG%, and share of her shots from each zone (Rim/Paint/Mid/Corner 3/Above-the-Break 3), from the same filtered games; it starts in rotation order, and any column header sorts it. FG% is shaded within this roster, so check At/G before reading much into a bright cell from a player with only a handful of attempts. Team Shooting/Defense by Zone reflect the same filters as everything else here (Games/Opponent-rank/etc.) -- zones (Rim/Paint/Mid-Range/Corner 3/Above-the-Break 3) are a distance/sideline heuristic from play-by-play shot locations, not an official ESPN zone definition; see the About note for how they're built and verified. A small number of games have no shot-location data available upstream and show as all zeros.
